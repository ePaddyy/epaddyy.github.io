I'm a **Data Scientist** and **machine learning engineer** in Accra, Ghana, with a degree in **Statistics with Computer Science** from the University of Ghana. That grounding shapes how I work. I care as much about how a model is evaluated as how it's built, and I'd rather report an honest number than an impressive one.

I've built regression and classification models on agricultural, automotive and telecom data. They include a **district-level maize yield model** that combines Ghanaian yield records with NASA satellite weather data. I've also built analytics dashboards, including one on a 286-person survey of Ghana's tech workforce, and worked with insurance data as a data analyst intern at Unique Insurance.

My current focus is the engineering side of ML: reproducible pipelines, time-aware validation, and serving models behind simple apps and APIs.
